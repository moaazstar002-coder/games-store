## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, dark-mode commerce and digital delivery ecosystem for core gamers, hardware enthusiasts, and digital collectors. The aesthetic marries the precision of competitive esports interfaces with the luxury polish of boutique digital storefronts.

### Aesthetic Persona & Direction
- **Tactile Cyber-Minimalism & Deep Slate Architecture**: Avoiding generic RGB clutter, the system deploys deep abyss slate backgrounds layered with precise surface tiers, razor-sharp 1px micro-borders, and electric violet accents.
- **Emotional Tone**: High-octane, trustworthy, immersive, and engineered. Interactions evoke zero latency and technical confidence—instilling trust for high-ticket hardware purchases and instant gratification for digital key releases.
- **Cross-Cultural Ready**: Layout metrics, typographic line heights, and directional components are engineered to mirror seamlessly for RTL locales (such as Arabic) without visual degradation or baseline clipping.

## Colors

The color system enforces a dark-first hierarchy engineered around luminous contrast on deep cold slate foundations.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F19`)**: Ground floor layer across all viewports.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#151B2B`)**: Primary structural cards, persistent headers, floating cart drawers, and category panels.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1E263C`)**: Active chip states, hovered table rows, search dropdowns, and modal dialogs.
- **Border Trim (`#26304A`)**: Universal 1px structural stroke applied to splitters, inputs, and container rims.

### Accent & Feedback Token Roles
- **Electric Violet (`#7C3AED`)**: Core brand anchor. Reserved for primary conversion triggers, checkout completions, hero highlights, and interactive toggles. Hover scales to `#8B5CF6`.
- **Neon Hot Rose (`#FF4D6D`)**: High-urgency alert and commercial modifier. Applied to discount pill tags (`-75%`), limited-time deal timers, and stock warnings.
- **Terminal Green (`#22C55E`)**: High-assurance operational state. Designated for instant key verification, in-stock indicators, online gamer tags, and digital redemption success.
- **Typography Neutrals**: `#F1F5F9` delivers primary content contrast against all surfaces; `#94A3B8` structures secondary subtitles, inactive states, and crossed-out historical prices.

## Typography

The type scale combines the industrial, technological punch of **Space Grotesk** for display headlines, telemetry, and numerical discount badges, paired with the structural legibility of **Outfit** for interface navigation, technical specifications, and commerce copy.

### Hierarchical Rules
- All numeric price displays, percentage badges, and product ratings use **Space Grotesk** with tabular numeric lining enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment in product grids.
- `label-sm` is uppercase-styled for platform chips (e.g., `PC`, `PS5`, `XBOX SERIES X`, `SWITCH`) with positive letter-spacing (`0.06em`) for maximum scan-speed.
- Line heights in Arabic and bidirectional contexts scale by 1.15x systematically on `body-md` and `body-lg` to prevent Arabic ascender/descender collisions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a controlled 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum container width of `1440px`.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Mechanics
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Product rails collapse into horizontal touch-swiping carousels with peek overflow (15% card edge visible).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. 2-column product catalog layout with collapsible sticky filter sheets.
- **Desktop (1024px – 1440px+)**: 12 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. 4-column storefront catalog with persistent left-hand dynamic filtering tree and floating cart flyout.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internals use strict binary-derived metrics: micro-spacing (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px) for icon-text inline pairings and badge padding; macro-spacing (`space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px) for separating catalog shelves and hero module banners.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages tonal stacking and targeted violet glow diffusions instead of traditional grey drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers & Border Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas)**: `#0B0F19` void background.
- **Layer 1 (Cards, Modules, Toolbars)**: `#151B2B` with a continuous `1px solid #26304A` stroke. This creates high-contrast definition without muddy drop-shadows.
- **Layer 2 (Popovers, Drawers, Flyouts)**: `#1E263C` with `1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.3)` border and ambient drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Luminescent Glow Accents
- Interactive focus states and high-priority primary banners utilize electric violet aura blooms: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.45)`.
- Critical flash-sale banners leverage rose auras: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.35)`.
- Glassmorphic panels (sticky top navbar and quick-search command palettes) apply `background: rgba(21, 27, 43, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a bottom border `1px solid rgba(38, 48, 74, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a balanced geometric form factor (Tier 2 - Rounded) with structural discipline. Core cards and panels employ `0.5rem` (8px) corner radiuses, escalating to `1rem` (16px) for hero containers and `1.5rem` (24px) for expansive modal surfaces.

### Component Curvature Mapping
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, Search Bars**: `0.5rem` (8px) for a modern, tactile feel.
- **Platform & Discount Badges**: `0.25rem` (4px) micro-rectangles conveying hardware angularity.
- **Category Chips, Status Dots, Avatar Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish contextual tags from interactive target cards.
- **Cover Artwork & Screenshots**: Nested with an inner radius of `6px` to maintain strict concentricity inside parent `8px` container cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Checkout, Pre-order)**: Solid `#7C3AED` background, `#FFFFFF` label, `0.5rem` radius, `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Hover elevates to `#8B5CF6` with an electric violet aura bloom. Active: slight scale down (`0.98`).
- **Secondary (Add to Wishlist, View Specs)**: Surface background `#151B2B`, `1px solid #26304A`, `#F1F5F9` text. Hover shifts border to `#7C3AED` and background to `#1E263C`.
- **Destructive / High Alert**: Outline or soft tint `#FF4D6D` with 15% opacity fill, transitioning to solid on critical interactions.

### Gaming Product Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#151B2B` with `1px solid #26304A`. 16:9 media container at the top featuring high-resolution game art or hardware rendering.
- **Overlay Elements**: Top-left discount badge (`#FF4D6D` background, `#FFFFFF` Space Grotesk bold text, e.g., `-60%`). Top-right quick-wishlist icon button with frosted glass backdrop blur.
- **Metadata Stack**: Platform badges grouped inline (PC, PS5, XBOX) using neutral fill `#1E263C` and `#94A3B8` typography. Game title in `headline-sm` `#F1F5F9`. Star ratings in neon amber/gold (`#FBBF24`) alongside review count.
- **Price Block**: Cross-out original price in `#94A3B8` `body-sm` juxtaposed with prominent active price in `#F1F5F9` `headline-sm`. Instant delivery checkmark in `#22C55E`.

### Platform & Status Badges
- **Platform Chips**: Ultra-compact, `label-sm` uppercase. Neutral gray-slate stroke with platform-specific branding accents during filter selection.
- **Verification & In-Stock**: Leading 6px pulsing dot in `#22C55E`, text reading "INSTANT DIGITAL CODE" or "IN STOCK - SHIPS TODAY" in `label-md` green.

### Input & Search Fields
- **Global Search Bar**: Elevated `#151B2B` with `#26304A` rim, `0.5rem` radius. Left-aligned gaming loupe icon, right-aligned hotkey badge (`CMD + K` or `CTRL + K` in `#1E263C` slate). Focus state deploys a sharp `#7C3AED` outer boundary ring without clipping layout borders.

### Category Chips
- Filter pills styled in `#151B2B` border `#26304A`. Hover shifts border to `#94A3B8`. Selected state toggles to `#7C3AED` fill with `#FFFFFF` bold typography.

### Responsive Navbar & Cart Flyout
- **Header**: Sticky `h-20` layout with frosted slate diffusion. Houses brand insignia, central high-speed search, regional/currency switcher, wishlist counter, and cart badge with electric violet quantity pill.
- **Cart Drawer**: Slides in from right (or left in RTL layouts), overlaying `#0B0F19` with a 60% alpha veil. Line-item rows feature game thumbnails, platform indicators, key platform selector dropdown, and a fixed bottom summary card containing coupon input and primary checkout trigger.
